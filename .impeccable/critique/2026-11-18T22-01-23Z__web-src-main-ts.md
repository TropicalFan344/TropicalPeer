## Priority issues

- [P1] The approval doesn't support a real decision. Notifications offer Accept with no warning or file names; the strip hides names past three; the visitor can't say who they are. Fix: no Accept on link-offer notifications, a Review files list, an unverified visitor note, clearer warning. /impeccable harden
- [P1] Expiry is reported as a refusal and counts toward the three declines. Fix: separate noanswer and limit codes, a visible deadline, Ask again keeping the files. /impeccable clarify
- [P1] Picking files sends the offer immediately. Fix: one review screen with remove, add, total, note and an explicit Ask to accept. /impeccable shape
- [P1] Several states say untrue things: visitor row during the prompt, one-person card while replying after download, "cancelled" toast when the page closed, "download stopped" for uploads, "weren't sent" after a partial batch, a retry button after replies were turned off. ## Receive-only link recommendation

Name it "Ask for files". A link has two abilities, gives and takes: Share files, Share files plus send back, Ask for files. Keep one New link button with a first question. Keep per-batch approval, without the three-minute expiry while the visitor's page is connected.
